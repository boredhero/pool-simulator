Camera

Move behind the cue ball toward the main group of your remaining balls (or the 8-Ball) after they stop, with the cue facing forward from your new view. On by default on phones; off on desktop. Moving the camera yourself takes priority.

Playback

Off by default. Plays the slow final rolls at 4× speed, with the same physics and shot result. Available with every ruleset.

Markings help you see the spin. Appearance only; all play identically.
